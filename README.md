## About Only Up!

Only Up! is a single-player 3D platformer where the goal is simple: climb as high as possible without falling. There's no combat and no real "story" beyond reaching the top — the challenge is entirely in the platforming, timing, and patience, since falling can send you a long way back down.

## Controls

- **Move**: WASD
- **Jump**: Space
- **Sprint**: Shift (use carefully — momentum affects jump distance)
- **Crouch**: Ctrl (helps with tight gaps and controlled landings)
- **Camera**: Mouse

## Tips for Climbing

- Take running jumps slowly at first — overshooting a landing is often worse than falling short.
- Save your progress often if the game allows checkpoints; falling can cost significant progress.
- Watch for moving or unstable platforms before committing to a jump.
- Crouching mid-air can help you land more precisely on small ledges.
- If you're stuck, look for alternate paths — many sections have more than one way up.

## FAQ

**Is Only Up! multiplayer?**
No, it's a single-player experience.

**What happens if I fall?**
Falling can send you back down significant portions of the tower, so cautious platforming is key.

**Is there an ending?**
Yes — reaching the top completes the climb.
